BREB offers best logistics solutions to their customer including warehousing, transports per river going barges, railway and truck plus using own vessel fleet for worldwide shipments.

Since 2019 BREB is acting also as freight forwarder with own trucks. Furthermore we are offering export and import clearance plus customs clearance.

BREB Forwarding-activities: - Weekly handling of paper cargo in reels via Cuxhaven, i.e. unloading trucks, intermediate warehousing, loading cargo on mafi trailers and shipment to Immingham.

- Intermodal Transport of wood pulp from any Baltic Country via Mukran Seaport, plus railway transport to European Hinterland (e.g. Italy, Romania, and Slovenia) and/or further shipments from Port of Koper to Mediterranean Sea, North Africa, Middle East, Far East and Black Sea.
