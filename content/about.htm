BREB GmbH & Co. KG, Cuxhaven arose from Bremer Reederei Eilemann & Bischoff GmbH, established 1951 in Bremen. We combine traditional Hanseatic ethics with state-of-the art technology and modern management-tools in all our activities.

BREB is a highly diversified Shipping Company with own Fleet-, own Chartering- and own Port Agency- and Port Logistics-division. Find more information: BREB Broschure

We provide our clients with effective, custom-tailored, and affordable sea transportation, employing a fleet of the newest highly sophisticated geared, box-shaped, open hatch vessels from 4.000 – 9.000 tons cargo capacity. Besides our own Fleet and Liner Services our Chartering-Desk finds the best solution utilizing any tonnage and Shipping Line and is supported by BREB-Forwarding, developing new ways of hinterland logistics by Rail, Truck or Barge.

We also deliver a full range of Liner-, Port-, Offshore-Agency from Zeebrugge to Sassnitz, as well as Warehousing and Stevedoring.

Blue Water BREB GmbH, a joint venture-firm of Danish Blue Water Shipping and BREB, serves the international Wind- and Oil&Gas-Industry in all German Ports with own departments in the two main German Offshore-hubs Cuxhaven for the North Sea and Sassnitz/Mukran for the Baltic Sea.
