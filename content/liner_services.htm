Conventional Services

BREB operates two own Liner-Services to North-Africa with weekly sailings from the Adriatic (BREB ADRIATIC PARCEL SERVICE) and monthly sailings from the North-Continent (BREB CONTINENTAL LINE).

Our Adriatic-Service includes all major Italian ports as well as Koper, Rijeka and Split. In the north, our ships call Scandinavian, German and ARAG-ports. Based on Breakbulk-cargoes like Forest-Products, Steel and bagged Cement we also ship Project Cargoes and Containers. Mainly for re-positioning we keep several Bulk-Contracts enabling our ships to sail in contract-based System-traffics.

Container Service

In close co-operation with Mukran Port Terminals BREB operates the Container-Line "Baltic-Sea-Bridge" between Mukran and Baltysk as integrated part of the new Silkroad from China to Europe. Since July 2019, BREB GmbH & Co. KG is a member of OTIF (Intergovernmental Organisation for International Carriage by Rail) and officially approved for the carriage of Containers under a continuous rail waybill (CIM) from Baltysk to Mukran and v.v..

More information at Baltic Sea Bridge or OTIF (EN).
