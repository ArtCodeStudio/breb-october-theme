BREB GmbH & Co. KG, Cuxhaven arose from Bremer Reederei Eilemann  & Bischoff GmbH, established 1951 in Bremen. We combine traditional  Hanseatic ethics with state-of-the art technology and modern  management-tools in all our activities.

BREB is a highly diversified Shipping Company with own Fleet-, own Chartering- and own Port Agency- and Port Logistics-division.

We  provide our clients with effective, custom-tailored, and affordable sea  transportation, employing a fleet of the newest highly sophisticated  geared, box-shaped, open hatch vessels from 4.000 – 9.000 tons cargo  capacity.
